description: When an active-learning model says it is uncertain, does that actually point us to the most useful next experiment? This paper compares ways of estimating and calibrating uncertainty across several materials-prediction models and tasks. It finds that better-looking calibration does not necessarily help a model learn from unfamiliar materials faster, highlighting the need to test uncertainty estimates in the discovery setting where they will be used.
