description: A high benchmark score is more useful when other researchers can reproduce it. This study revisits ALIGNN, a graph neural network for predicting materials properties, and repeats its tests across a broad set of crystal-property tasks. The results largely agree with the original report while also showing the small differences that can arise from randomness, data splits and computing hardware.
