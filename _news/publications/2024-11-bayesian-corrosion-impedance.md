description: Electrochemical impedance spectra are often measured to very low frequencies, even though those measurements can take a long time. Using simulated corrosion data and Bayesian analysis, this study asks when common equivalent-circuit models are supported by the data. It also tests how removing low-frequency points changes the inferred parameters, suggesting checks that could help decide when a shorter measurement is sufficient.
