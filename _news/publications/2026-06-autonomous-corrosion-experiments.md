description: Corrosion experiments often take substantial hands-on time, making it hard to build the datasets needed for materials discovery. This paper introduces MAP-E, a platform that combines robotic liquid handling, sample transfer and parallel electrochemical measurements. The team checks its reproducibility against a corrosion-testing benchmark, then uses uncertainty-guided sampling to map how pH and chloride affect 304 stainless steel.
