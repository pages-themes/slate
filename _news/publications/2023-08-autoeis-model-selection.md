description: Interpreting electrochemical impedance data often depends on an expert choosing a plausible equivalent circuit. This paper introduces AutoEIS, which searches circuit structures automatically and uses Bayesian analysis to assess them. The authors test it on data from oxygen-evolution catalysis, alloy corrosion and carbon-dioxide reduction, illustrating how automated model selection can support different electrochemical studies.
