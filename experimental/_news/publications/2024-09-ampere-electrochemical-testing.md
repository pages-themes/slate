description: Preparing catalyst samples and measuring them by hand can slow electrochemical screening and add variability. This paper presents AMPERE, which brings robotic liquid handling and modular electrochemical reactors into one workflow. The team uses it to study how catalyst-ink formulation changes activity and stability measurements for oxygen-evolution catalysts, showing why preparation details matter when comparing materials. Jehad Abed and Yang Bai are credited as equal first authors.
