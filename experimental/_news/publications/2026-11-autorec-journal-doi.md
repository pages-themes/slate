description: >-
  AutoREC is an open-source Python platform for developing reinforcement learning agents that propose equivalent circuit models from electrochemical impedance spectroscopy data. Its reference agent learns to modify circuit structures and uses feedback from fitting to guide its next steps. The paper demonstrates the workflow on synthetic spectra and on experimental data from batteries, corrosion, oxygen evolution, and carbon dioxide reduction, while discussing the limits of applying a trained agent to unfamiliar experiments. The journal DOI is now registered for a 2027 issue.
