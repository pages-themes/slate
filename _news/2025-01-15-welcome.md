# Welcome to the AutoDIAL Website

Welcome to the website of the Autonomous Discovery of Alloys (AutoDIAL) research group at the University of Toronto's Department of Materials Science and Engineering. We created this space to share the group's research, publications, and activities.

## What to Expect

This news section shares updates about:

- **Research**: Projects and developments from the lab
- **Publications**: New papers and research findings
- **Group Activities**: Conferences, events, and other group updates
- **People**: Achievements and contributions from group members

Explore the site to learn more about the group and its work in autonomous materials discovery.
