description: Active learning can save experiments, but conventional models often need task-specific features and enough initial data to get started. This study tests whether a pretrained language model can choose promising experiments from text descriptions without additional training. Across four materials datasets, its iterative search reached top-performing candidates with fewer trials than the conventional models tested; the paper also examines how prompt format and run-to-run variation affect the results.
