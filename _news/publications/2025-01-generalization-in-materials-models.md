description: How well does a materials model work on genuinely unfamiliar chemistry or crystal structures? This paper examines common out-of-distribution tests and finds that many supposedly new test examples still lie near the training data in representation space. The harder cases show where performance drops and where simply training longer or on more data may not solve the problem.
