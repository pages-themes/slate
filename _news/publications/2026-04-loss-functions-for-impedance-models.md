description: 'Fitting an equivalent-circuit model to impedance data depends on the loss function used to compare the model and measurement. The authors compare established choices with two new functions based on the Bode representation, using simulated spectra and several measures of fit and parameter accuracy. Their results show a trade-off: one weighting gives the strongest overall fit, while a log-based option can be faster and recover many circuit parameters more accurately.'
